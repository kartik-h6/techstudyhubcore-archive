# Future Rebrand

## The principle

> **Do not immediately create a replacement domain.**

**[LESSON]** A future rebrand should not simply recreate:

```text
old domain → new domain → same subdomains → same maintenance problem
```

## The correct order

```text
validate future need
→ establish operating capacity
→ establish actual team / collaborators
→ define organisation
→ define product
→ choose identity
→ choose domain
→ build infrastructure
```

Identity and domain come *after* product and organisation — not before.

## Why

**[LESSON]** A future team should inherit a **documented system**, rather than a domain kept alive only because of sunk cost.

## Guidance for a next identity

- A **personal** root identity for personal assets, so personal presence never depends on a brand that might end
- A brand identity only when a brand genuinely exists
- No subdomain sprawl
