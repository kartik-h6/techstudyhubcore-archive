# Future Team

## The lesson that shapes this file

> **A team cannot be treated as an assumption.**

**[LESSON]** The previous attempt planned around collaborators who never confirmed. The next attempt should assume solo, and treat a team as an accelerator if and when it appears.

## What a real team would need

- **Named owners per area** — no area without a maintainer
- **A shared definition of done** — what *maintained* means
- **Written decisions** — so the reasoning survives turnover; this archive is the template
- **A scope budget** — the number of products should not exceed the number of owners

## How teams actually form

**[LESSON]** Around visible momentum, not around an invitation. The way to attract collaborators is to have public, competent, ongoing work.
