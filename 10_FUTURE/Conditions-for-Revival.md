# Conditions for Revival

A revival should be a decision made against criteria, not an impulse.

## Conditions

A future continuation should ideally have:

1. a clearly defined problem
2. evidence of user need
3. a sustainable operating model
4. defined ownership
5. a real team or reliable collaborators
6. documented architecture
7. security and privacy requirements
8. a deliberate brand and domain decision

## The rule

> **Preserve the possibility without creating an obligation.**

**[LESSON]** Do not assume future continuation.

## The trigger question

> What has changed since last time?

If the honest answer is *nothing*, a revival will reproduce the same ending.
