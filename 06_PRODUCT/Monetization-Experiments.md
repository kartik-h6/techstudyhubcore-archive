# Monetization Experiments

## What was explored

**[EXPERIMENT]** The Digital Setup concept was the monetization-adjacent area — a local-business digital infrastructure service rather than a content product.

Historical pricing concepts (never current commercial offers):

| Tier | Concept price |
|---|---|
| Foundation | ₹1,999 |
| Customer Flow | ₹4,999 |
| Market Presence | ₹9,999 |

The framing was deliberately outcome-oriented: sell inquiries, trust, visibility and connected customer flow — not websites.

## The core lesson

> **Do not monetize unfinished value.**

**[LESSON]** Monetization was attempted on value that was not yet finished or validated. That is a common and costly ordering mistake. Core value should exist before payment infrastructure becomes a priority.
