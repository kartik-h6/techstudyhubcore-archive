# Product Philosophy

## As it evolved

**[FACT]** Early framing: build useful things for students.
Later framing: build a platform with multiple areas.

## What the evolution revealed

**[LESSON]** The second framing was an organisational ambition disguised as a product philosophy. It required a team, but was adopted while solo.

## The philosophy worth keeping

- **Outcome over technology.** Sell or serve the outcome, not the tooling.
- **Validate before scaling.** Generating pages is not proof that people need those pages.
- **Architecture before mass content.** Define the structure before producing volume.
- **Do not monetize unfinished value.** Core value should exist before payment infrastructure becomes a priority.
- **Activity is not traction.** Commits, pages and deployments are activity.
- **Maintenance is product design.** A product one person cannot responsibly maintain has an operating-model problem, even if its code works.
- **Do not build around hypothetical team members.**
