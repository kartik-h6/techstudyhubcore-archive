## How to capture

1. Open the registrar account and record ownership and expiry details.
2. Open the Cloudflare dashboard → the domain → DNS → Records.
3. Export or screenshot the full record list.
4. Record each record above, redacting sensitive values.
5. Update the capture metadata and status.
