# Why Retiring

## The decision

**[FACT]** The public infrastructure of TechStudyHubCore — the domain and its subdomains — is being retired rather than renewed indefinitely.

## The reasons

The author is now:

- pursuing **M.Pharm (Pharmacy Practice)** at RIPER, Anantapur
- working on **PMAS**
- handling academic responsibilities
- developing a research and professional direction
- continuing to learn healthcare technology and AI

**[LESSON]** The TechStudyHubCore ecosystem had become too broad to responsibly maintain as a solo person. A team was hoped for, but team formation was not confirmed.

## What is being retired, and what is not

> The public infrastructure is being retired.
>
> The knowledge is not being retired.
>
> The repositories are not being treated as worthless.
>
> The learning is not being discarded.
>
> The domain may expire.
>
> The engineering experience remains.

## What retirement is not

- It is not a statement that the project was worthless.
- It is not a statement that everything failed.
- It is not the end of the skills — those are permanent.

## The framing

The retirement is primarily a **focus and operating-capacity decision**, not a verdict on the work.
