# Shutdown Checklist

Steps for retiring the public infrastructure cleanly.

- [ ] **Export content** — articles, notes and material from each area before the domain lapses. Content outlives domains; a folder does not expire.
- [ ] **Export the DNS configuration** — a copy of the records, kept in this archive for reference.
- [ ] **Re-host the personal portfolio** independently of the brand domain (personal hosting or a personal address).
- [ ] **Update all links** pointing at the brand domain — see `Domain-Dependency-Checklist.md`.
- [ ] **Record the final state** — see `Final-State.md`.
- [ ] **Keep the repositories** — no action; they do not expire with the domain.
- [ ] **Announce or update bios** where the brand URL is used.

## Note on redirects

**[FACT]** Because the domain is not being renewed, no redirect can be configured after expiry. The transition must be handled by updating links at their sources.
