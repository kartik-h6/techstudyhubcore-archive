# Product Lessons

**[LESSON]**

## Outcome over technology

For Digital Setup the principle became: **sell outcomes, not websites** — inquiries, trust, visibility, presence, connected customer flow.

## Validate before scaling

Generating pages is not proof that people need those pages.

## Architecture before mass content

Define the information architecture before generating hundreds of pages.

## Do not monetize unfinished value

Core value should exist before payment infrastructure becomes a priority.

## Activity is not traction

Commits, pages, deployments and experiments are activity. They are not automatically proof of sustained user value.

## Falsification

Ask: What would prove this wrong? What metric determines whether I continue? How can I test this in seven days? What is the opportunity cost? If I could build only one thing, what should it be?

## Maintenance is product design

A product that one person cannot responsibly maintain has an operating-model problem, even if its code works.

## Do not build around hypothetical team members

A team cannot be treated as guaranteed future capacity.
