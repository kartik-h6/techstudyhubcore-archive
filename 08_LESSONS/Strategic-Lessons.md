# Strategic Lessons

**[LESSON]**

## Operating capacity is the real constraint

The limiting factor was never tooling. It was the number of things one person could responsibly maintain alongside a full-time academic programme.

## Scope is what you must maintain

Not what you announce, and not what you can technically deploy.

## A team cannot be an assumption

Planning around collaborators who have not committed is planning around nothing. The next attempt should assume solo and treat a team as an accelerator.

## Opportunity cost matters

Time spent maintaining a wide, low-traction ecosystem is time not spent on the work directly connected to the author's academic and professional direction.

## Infrastructure is not the problem

When infrastructure becomes the focus, the underlying problem — whether anyone needs the thing — stops being examined.

## Sunk cost is not a reason

A domain kept alive only because it was already paid for is not a strategy.

## Deliberate retirement is a decision

Ending something intentionally, and documenting why, is more valuable than a quiet disappearance.
