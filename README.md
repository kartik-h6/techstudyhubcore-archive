# TechStudyHubCore — Archive

> **The domain may expire. The knowledge and engineering experience do not.**

An archival record of the complete TechStudyHubCore journey — what it was, why it existed, what was built, why the public infrastructure is now being retired, what remains, and what could happen in the future.

This is not a success story. It is also not a failure story. It is the record of an independent technical and product learning ecosystem, documented honestly — experiments, mistakes, corrections, learning, decisions, transition.

## What TechStudyHubCore was

An independent, solo-built digital learning ecosystem, created by a pharmacy student with no formal computer-science background.

Its central philosophy was:

> **Information ≠ Understanding.**

And, more broadly:

> **Technology should enhance, not replace human care.**

The intended audience included pharmacy students, nursing students, school-level science students, and learners preparing for examinations.

## Why it existed

The project began from a practical question: how can learning material become clearer, more structured, visual, searchable and useful to students — rather than simply becoming another collection of notes?

The initial direction was a clarity-first digital learning environment.

## What was built

- A **root gateway** (front door and routing layer)
- A **Pharmacy / B.Pharm** structured learning architecture
- **SSLC** content as a second learning destination
- A **Digital Setup** concept for local-business digital infrastructure
- A **personal portfolio** (professional identity layer)
- A **CGPA Calculator** utility
- **TrailCode** — the early prototype and learning artifact
- An **AI-assisted development workflow** (Ollama, Qwen2.5-Coder, Continue.dev, Gemini tooling, prompt engineering)
- Deployment and infrastructure experience: Git/GitHub, Netlify, Vercel exploration, Cloudflare DNS, custom domains, subdomains

## Why it is being retired

The public infrastructure is being retired because the author is now:

- pursuing **M.Pharm (Pharmacy Practice)** at RIPER, Anantapur
- working on **PMAS**
- handling academic responsibilities
- developing a research and professional direction in pharmacy practice and digital health

As a solo person, the ecosystem became too broad to responsibly maintain. A team was hoped for, but team formation was never confirmed.

**The public infrastructure is being retired. The knowledge is not.**
