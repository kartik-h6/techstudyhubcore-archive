# Decisions

Key decisions, with the reasoning preserved — not only the outcome.

## Why not simply keep renewing the domain?

Not because domains are expensive.

Because the **maintenance obligation had become disproportionate to the current purpose of the platform**.

The distinction matters: the constraint was obligation, not cost.

## Why wasn't everything migrated to Vercel?

Because changing hosting wouldn't solve the underlying operational problem.

## Why wasn't another domain immediately purchased?

Because rebranding without changing the operating model would recreate the same problem.

## Why preserve GitHub?

Because the intellectual and technical assets have continuing value even when the public infrastructure doesn't.

## Why does PMAS take priority?

Because it is directly connected to the author's M.Pharm (Pharmacy Practice) work and current research and product direction.
