# Prompt Engineering

## What was learned

**[LESSON]**

- Clear context and constraints produce usable output; vague prompts produce noise
- Iteration beats one-shot prompting
- Prompts for code, content and design are different disciplines
- Reviewing AI output is itself a skill — and the one that actually protects quality

## Why it matters for the future

**[FACT]** Prompting became a transferable professional skill, formalised through Gen AI certification, and is directly relevant to medical writing, documentation, and product work.

## The core lesson

> The value is not in the prompt. The value is in knowing what a good result looks like.
