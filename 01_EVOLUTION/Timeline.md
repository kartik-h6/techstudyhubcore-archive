## Phases

**[FACT]** The project moved through recognisable phases rather than a single plan:

1. **Early phase** — learning HTML/CSS/JS by building a prototype
2. **TrailCode phase** — the prototype became interactive; client-side concepts explored
3. **Version-control phase** — Git and GitHub introduced; deployment became repeatable
4. **Infrastructure phase** — Netlify, a custom domain, Cloudflare DNS
5. **Ecosystem phase** — subdomains and multiple independent projects (gateway, Pharmacy, SSLC, Digital Setup, portfolio, calculator)
6. **Content-architecture phase** — structured hierarchy, resources attached to topic pages
7. **AI-assisted phase** — local and hosted AI tools joined the workflow
8. **Maintenance phase** — the operating cost of the ecosystem became visible
9. **Retirement phase** — decision to preserve knowledge and retire public infrastructure

## The transformation

As the platform evolved, the working intent shifted from:

> *I am building websites.*

toward:

> *I am building systems of connected products.*

That shift is the central story of the project.
