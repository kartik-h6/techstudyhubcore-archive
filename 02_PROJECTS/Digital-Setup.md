# Digital Setup

## What it was

**[EXPERIMENT]** Digital Setup explored a local-business digital infrastructure / service concept.

## The core idea

**[LESSON]** Do not simply sell websites. Sell **outcomes**:

- customer inquiries
- trust
- visibility
- digital presence
- connected customer flow

## The concept included

- landing page
- WhatsApp integration
- Google presence
- local SEO basics
- map integration
- digital infrastructure

## The 3D / WebGL design direction

**[FUTURE IDEA]** A later design direction explored a scroll-driven 3D/WebGL cinematic experience.

The architectural idea was:

```text
scroll
→ camera state
→ 3D world state
→ editorial state
→ next chapter
```

The principle: the 3D world itself should communicate the transformation, rather than acting as a decorative background.
