# SSLC

## What it was

**[FACT]** SSLC content was prepared as another learning destination, intended to remain accessible through the gateway.

It extended the audience beyond pharmacy toward school-level students.

## The product lesson it demonstrated

**[LESSON]** A content product can be ready or useful *without* that automatically meaning its infrastructure should be maintained forever.

Readiness and maintenance obligation are separate questions. SSLC made that distinction visible.

## Status

**[EXPERIMENT]** Preserved here as one of the major experiments of the ecosystem. Retired with the public infrastructure.
