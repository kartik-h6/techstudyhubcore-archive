# Portfolio

## What it was

**[FACT]** The portfolio functioned as a professional identity layer within the ecosystem.

It demonstrated:

- work
- skills
- technical learning
- professional direction

## Why it was part of the ecosystem

At the time, the portfolio was treated as another destination on the brand's domain.

## The lesson

**[LESSON]** A personal portfolio is a personal asset. Tying it to a brand domain means the personal presence inherits the brand's lifecycle risk.

When the brand domain is retired, the portfolio URL is retired with it — unless it is re-hosted independently.

> Personal identity should never depend on a brand that might end.
