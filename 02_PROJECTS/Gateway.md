# Gateway

## What it was

**[FACT]** The root gateway was the front door of the ecosystem — intended as a routing layer, not a giant content site.

Its purpose: let a visitor understand what the platform was and reach the appropriate product quickly.

## The concept included

- navigation
- hero
- routing cards
- why the platform exists
- transparency
- philosophy
- contact
- footer

## Why a gateway rather than one large site

**[LESSON]** A gateway makes sense when the destinations behind it are strong. If the destinations cannot be maintained, the gateway amplifies the problem instead of solving it — it advertises more than the operator can sustain.
